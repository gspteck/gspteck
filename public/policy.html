Data categories we collect

Information from third parties

Users should never provide passwords, private keys, wallet recovery phrases, seed phrases, financial credentials, or government ID numbers through the Services unless explicitly required and authorized.

How we use information

We use personal data to:

  • Provide, operate, secure, and maintain the apps and websites you use
  • Create and manage accounts; personalize experiences
  • Process subscriptions, rewards, or account deletion requests
  • Deliver customer support and respond to abuse reports

We do not sell personal information as a standalone product. Some free tiers may show ads via third-party networks that collect device advertising IDs under their own terms. Where permitted by law, anonymized or aggregated datasets that no longer reasonably identify you may continue to be used for analytics and product development after a deletion request.

Where GDPR or similar laws apply, we process information under consent, contract performance, legal obligation, vital interests, and legitimate business interests — including operating the Services, improving products, analytics, personalization, marketing measurement, fraud detection, security, R&D, and (where lawful) commercialization of aggregated or de-identified information.

Data retention

Bright Data network (optional participation)

Participation in the Bright Data network via the Bright SDK is entirely optional and is not required to use the Services or access their core functionality. Access to premium features does not depend on participation in the Bright Data network.

In return for premium features of CoinDrop, you may choose to be a peer on the Bright Data network. By doing so, you agree to have read and accepted the Bright SDK End User License Agreement (EULA) and Bright Data Privacy Policy.

Opt-out can be completed by disabling the “Bright Data Network” setting in Tasks > Web indexing inside CoinDrop.
