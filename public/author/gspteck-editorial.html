GSPTeck editorial team

Articles on gspteck.com with the byline “GSPTeck Editorial” are written and maintained by the GSPTeck editorial team at GSPTeck, giosaia's indie studio building mobile and web apps with Flutter and Firebase. Articles bylined “giosaia (GSPTeck)” are written by giosaia.

The guides cover shipping and running Flutter apps on Firebase as a solo developer: Google Play closed testing, staged rollouts, target API deadlines, Data safety forms and Android vitals, plus Firestore costs, security rules and backups, Cloud Functions secrets, App Check, Remote Config and Crashlytics. Every article shows in its byline the date it was published and the date its facts were last verified.
